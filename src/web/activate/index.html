Get your Book Studio Pro key

Your key is made from your purchase, shown here once it is ready, and checked on your own device from then on. Book Studio never phones home.

Looking up your purchase…

It is in the link you were sent back to after paying (checkout_id=…), and in Polar's receipt email.

Your key

Use it

  1. Obsidian: Settings → Book Studio → License key → paste it and press Activate, or Import file.
  2. Web app: open the license panel and paste it, or import the file.

The key never expires and works offline. Keep the key itself, or your checkout ID from the receipt: asking again with the same ID gives the same key. If you need help, reply to your Polar receipt.
