Seven small tools that answer the questions every paperback author asks before uploading to Amazon KDP. Each one runs in your browser: nothing you type is sent anywhere, and once a page has loaded it keeps working without a connection. The numbers come from KDP's own help pages, and every page says the day they were last checked.

{{tools}}

## What to do after the numbers

The tools tell you what size and how many pages. [Book Studio](app:) does the rest for free: it takes your Word or Markdown manuscript, sets it in the size you chose with the margins KDP requires, and gives you a print-ready PDF, an accessible EPUB and the full wraparound cover. Every tool ends with a button that opens it with your size and paper already set.
