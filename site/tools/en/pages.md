## How it estimates

The tool works out how many lines of your type size fit in the text block (the page minus the margins, with room for a running head and the page number), how many characters fit on a line, and at about six characters per word, how many words fit on a page. It takes 10% off for the short last line of each paragraph, adds a page and a half for every chapter start, adds the front and back matter you give it, and rounds up to a whole sheet, which is two pages. The inside margin KDP requires depends on the page count, so it settles that with a second pass.

This is an estimate. For prose it lands close (it gives 140 pages for the sample edition of *The Wonderful Wizard of Oz*, which Book Studio sets in 140), but poetry and books with many short lines run longer. A real count depends on hyphenation, how many scene breaks and headings you have, and whether chapters begin on a right-hand page.

## From a guess to the real number

Once you know roughly how many pages you have, the [spine width](tool:spine), the [cover size](tool:cover) and the [printing cost](tool:cost) follow. To get the exact page count, [open Book Studio](app:), paste or import the manuscript and read it from the layout: it is the same engine that produces the print PDF.
