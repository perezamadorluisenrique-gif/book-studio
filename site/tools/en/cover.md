## The formula

KDP's help page gives it in two lines:

- **Cover width** = bleed + back cover width + spine width + front cover width + bleed
- **Cover height** = bleed + trim height + bleed

The bleed is 0.125 in (3.2 mm). It is added to the top, bottom and outside edges, which is why a 6 × 9 in book with a 0.6756 in spine needs a cover of 12.9256 × 9.25 in. The [spine width calculator](tool:spine) explains where the spine comes from.

## Using the template

The PDF and PNG show, to scale: the **bleed** (pink, which is cut off), the **trim line** (red, where the book is cut), the **fold lines** of the spine (blue), the **safe area** for text (green dashed, 0.125 in inside the trim, which is KDP's minimum) and, when the book has 79 pages or more, the **spine text area**. The barcode box is a guide: KDP's help page does not publish its size, so the template uses the 2 × 1.2 in box of KDP's own cover template. If you leave the area empty, KDP places the barcode for you.

Before you export your cover, remember KDP's checklist: one PDF with back, spine and front together, fonts embedded, transparencies flattened, at least 300 dpi, and no crop marks or template text left in the file. Delete this template's labels and colored guides before you upload.
