## How KDP works out the spine

KDP gives one formula: **page count × the thickness of one page**. The thickness depends only on the paper:

| Paper | Inches per page | Millimetres per page |
|---|---|---|
| White paper, black and white | 0.002252 | 0.0572 |
| Cream paper, black and white | 0.0025 | 0.0635 |
| Standard color | 0.002252 | 0.0572 |
| Premium color | 0.002347 | 0.0596 |

A 300-page book on white paper has a spine of 300 × 0.002252 = 0.6756 inches. Page count means pages, not sheets: a sheet has two pages, and the numbers above already account for that.

## Text on the spine

KDP only prints text on the spine of books with at least 79 pages (the Cover Creator asks for 80). Keep the text at least 0.0625 in (1.6 mm) away from each edge of the spine, and allow 0.0625 in of variance on either side of the fold lines, because every book varies slightly when it is bound.
