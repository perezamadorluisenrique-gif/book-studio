## How the numbers work

**Printing cost** = fixed cost + page count × cost per page. On Amazon.com, in US dollars:

| Book | Pages | Regular trim | Large trim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Black ink, white or cream paper | 24–110 | $2.30 flat | $2.84 flat |
| Black ink, white or cream paper | 111–828 | $1.00 + $0.012 per page | $1.00 + $0.017 per page |
| Premium color | 24–40 | $3.60 flat | $4.20 flat |
| Premium color | 42–828 | $1.00 + $0.065 per page | $1.00 + $0.08 per page |
| Standard color | 72–600 | $1.00 + $0.0255 per page | $1.00 + $0.0402 per page |

A *large trim* is wider than 6.12 in or taller than 9 in. Trim size, bleed and cover finish otherwise do not change the cost. KDP's own example: a 300-page black-ink paperback costs $1.00 + 300 × $0.012 = $4.60 to print.

**Royalty** = royalty rate × list price − printing cost. On Amazon.com the rate is 50% for list prices up to $9.98 and 60% from $9.99. Copies sold through Expanded Distribution pay 40% of the list price, minus the printing cost. KDP's worked example: a $15 book of 333 pages in black ink earns (0.60 × $15) − $5.00 = $4.00.

**Lowest list price** = printing cost ÷ royalty rate, so that a sale never loses you money. The highest price KDP accepts on Amazon.com is $250.

## What this does not cover

Figures are for Amazon.com in US dollars. Other Amazon stores have their own costs, rates and taxes. Prices can change: the date above is when these numbers were last read from KDP's help pages. KDP's pricing page shows the final figure for your book.
