## KDP's margin rules

The inside margin (the gutter) grows with the page count, because a thicker book curves more at the spine:

| Pages | Inside margin |
|---|---|
| 24 to 150 | 0.375 in (9.6 mm) |
| 151 to 300 | 0.5 in (12.7 mm) |
| 301 to 500 | 0.625 in (15.9 mm) |
| 501 to 700 | 0.75 in (19.1 mm) |
| 701 to 828 | 0.875 in (22.3 mm) |

The top, bottom and outside margins must be at least 0.25 in (6.4 mm) in a book without bleed, and 0.375 in (9.6 mm) in a book with bleed. They do not have to be equal.

## Bleed

A book has bleed when something printed, such as a picture or a colored band, reaches the edge of the page. Then the page you export must be larger than the trim: add 0.125 in (3.2 mm) to the top, bottom and outside edge. A 6 × 9 in book is exported as 6.125 × 9.25 in. A book of plain text has no bleed and is exported at the trim size.
