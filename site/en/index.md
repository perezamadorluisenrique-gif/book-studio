Book Studio turns a folder of Markdown notes into a print-ready PDF for Amazon KDP paperbacks and an EPUB 3. It runs in your browser. Your manuscript is never uploaded, and there is no account, no cookies and no analytics. The web app is free and MIT licensed.

## Try it

[Open the app](app:) and it loads the sample book, *The Wonderful Wizard of Oz*, so you can see the result before you choose a folder. When you are ready, open the folder with your own notes and export.
