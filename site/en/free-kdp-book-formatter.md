Book Studio is a free KDP book formatter. You give it Markdown notes and it gives you a print-ready PDF interior for a KDP paperback and an EPUB 3 ebook. It runs in your browser, so you can [open the app](app:) and try it now.

## What free means here

The web app is free, MIT licensed and open source. These limits do not exist:

- No account and no sign-up.
- No watermark on the PDF or the EPUB.
- No page limit and no book limit in the web app.
- No time limit.

It also collects nothing. There are no cookies and no analytics. Your manuscript stays on your device, and the web app keeps only your settings in the browser's local storage.

## What the free plan includes

The free plan prints three trims: 6 × 9 in, KDP's most common paperback size, plus 5 × 8 and 5.5 × 8.5. It includes the Classic and Modern themes, the print PDF and the EPUB. Other trim sizes and themes are covered in the Pro section below.

## What the PDF does for you

KDP wants a print-ready PDF with embedded fonts and the right inside margin for your page count. Book Studio handles those parts:

- The inside margin grows with your page count to KDP's minimum. See [KDP trim sizes, margins and bleed](guide:kdp-trim-sizes) for the table.
- Fonts are embedded.
- The book is padded to KDP's 24-page minimum.
- Bleed is optional.
- It reports the spine width, so your cover designer has the number.

The typesetting is meant to look like a real book. Lines are broken with the Knuth–Plass algorithm, the one TeX uses. Hyphenation works in English (US and UK), Spanish, French, German, Italian and Portuguese. You also get smart quotes and dashes, widows and orphans avoided, drop caps, ornamental scene breaks, chapter openers, part pages and running heads. Chapters can open on a right-hand page.

## Front and back matter

You do not build these by hand. Book Studio makes the half title, title page, copyright page (ISBN, edition, publisher), dedication, epigraph, table of contents and an "Also by" page. Footnotes are collected as endnotes under "Notes".

## The EPUB

The EPUB 3 uses semantic XHTML, a navigable table of contents, landmarks, embedded fonts and a cover. It sets the book's language on every page. Both sample books pass W3C EPUBCheck 5.2.1 with no errors or warnings. The details are in [the accessible EPUB guide](guide:accessible-epub).

## How you write the book

You write in any Markdown editor. Obsidian works well. One "book note" lists your chapters as links, and the app does the rest. [From Markdown to a book](guide:markdown-to-book) shows the format. If you want the whole path in order, follow [how to format a book for KDP](guide:format-a-book-for-kdp).

## Check the output before you trust it

Download a finished sample and look at it. [Download the sample PDF](download:oz/pdf) of *The Wonderful Wizard of Oz*, or the [Spanish sample PDF](download:leyendas/pdf). Every sample PDF passes Book Studio's own KDP check on every build: page size equals trim, inside margin for the page count, all fonts embedded, 24–828 pages.

In the app, a live paged preview shows facing pages and zoom. A Problems tab lists broken links and missing images with the note and line.

## What it does not do

There is no built-in word processor. You write elsewhere. The free plan has no cover designer, only the spine width. It prints three trims. And it is a young project by one developer. If you are comparing tools, [Book Studio against Vellum, Atticus and Kindle Create](guide:vellum-atticus-kindle-create) is an honest side-by-side.
