## Inside margin depends on page count

The inside margin, also called the gutter, is the one next to the spine. The thicker the book, the more of the page the binding eats, so KDP asks for more.

| Page count | Minimum inside margin |
|---|---|
| 24–150 pages | 0.375 in (9.6 mm) |
| 151–300 pages | 0.5 in (12.7 mm) |
| 301–500 pages | 0.625 in (15.9 mm) |
| 501–700 pages | 0.75 in (19.1 mm) |
| 701–828 pages | 0.875 in (22.3 mm) |

Book Studio never lets the inside margin fall below KDP's minimum for your page count, and checks it again as the book grows or shrinks. The themes start from a comfortable margin (0.75 in on the 142-page Oz sample) and widen it when a long book needs more.

## Outside, top and bottom margins

| Layout | Minimum outside, top and bottom margin |
|---|---|
| No bleed | 0.25 in (6.4 mm) |
| With bleed | 0.375 in (9.6 mm) |

These are minimums. Body text usually sits comfortably inside them.

## Bleed

Bleed is extra image area that runs past the trim edge so no white sliver shows after cutting. You need it only when something, such as an image or a background color, touches the edge of the page. KDP's bleed is 0.125 in (3.2 mm) on the top, bottom and outside edges.

The page size with bleed is the trim width plus 0.125 in and the trim height plus 0.25 in. A 6 × 9 book becomes 6.125 × 9.25. A book that is all text inside normal margins does not need bleed. In Book Studio, bleed is optional.

## Page count limits

| Paper and ink | Page count |
|---|---|
| Black ink on white paper, most trims | 24–828 |
| Black ink on white paper, 8.25 × 6 and 8.25 × 8.25 | 24–800 |
| Black ink on white paper, 8.5 × 8.5 and 8.5 × 11 | 24–590 |
| Black ink on white paper, 8.27 × 11.69 | 24–780 |
| Cream paper, most trims | 24–776 |
| Standard color | 72–600 |

Book Studio pads a short book to KDP's 24-page minimum and reports the spine width, which your cover designer needs.
